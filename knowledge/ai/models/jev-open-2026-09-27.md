## What it is

A snapshot of **downloadable** System One / Jev-class decision
models as of 2026-09-27. Official TypeSafe Jev remains API-only and
is not listed as a download.

JevBench composite (Intelligence, Calibration, Speed, Cost) is the
independent board. Public-slice accuracies and author benches are
marked as such. Do not treat an author number as a board rank.

## When to read

Picking a local typed-decision model for NVIDIA or Apple Silicon.
Re-fetch the live board before any adoption decision. For a FreeCAD
executor, see Adjacent — that row is not a JevBench substitute.

## State of play (2026-09-27)

TypeSafe Jev 1.13.0 is still the hosted reference (`jev-latest`).
On JevBench v1.4.2.2 it sits **#4 at 63.29**. Three downloadable
open rows sit above it on that composite:

| Rank | System | JevBench Score |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Imajev-4B | 67.37 |
| 2 | Plumb-4B | 65.84 |
| 3 | decider-4b v2 | 64.13 |
| 4 | Jev 1.13.0 (hosted) | 63.29 |
| 5 | JevK5 v0.2.0 | 62.04 |

The board had 95 systems / 91 ranked in that revision. Highest
Intelligence on the board is still large generative models; the
harmonic mean does not let raw accuracy buy back weak Speed or Cost.

Community chatter (X + Hub, last ~10 days):

- Imajev-4B and Plumb-4B are the rows people cite when they say an
  open 4B beats hosted Jev on the **composite** board.
- Winnow-12B Q8 is the row people cite for a public-231 **tie** with
  Jev 1.13 (198/231). Composite rank is lower because of size/cost.
- CLM-8B is the loud new name. Frozen Qwen3-8B encoder + small
  contrastive heads (state and action). Primary links:
  [Notion blog](https://contrastive-lm.notion.site),
  [code](https://github.com/Contrastive-LM/CLM),
  [Hub org](https://huggingface.co/Contrastive-LM),
  [Discord](https://discord.gg/5dAQEDJBs). **Author-side** until a
  JevBench row lands — not board ranks: up to ~9× faster than Jev
  (Hub: ~13× with ~1k cached action candidates); DeepSWE 81.6% and
  Terminal-Bench 2.1 87.6% as a **fine-tuned verifier**. The Hub
  card says those agentic numbers need fine-tuned heads, not the
  `CLM-v0.1-8B` checkpoint zero-shot. Author also claims Jev fails
  as a verifier for long-horizon agentic coding; that is the
  author’s framing, not a JevBench finding.
- Laya and Julia-1 are the viral tiny models. Fast. Weak on hard /
  many-label slices versus the 4B class.
- `openjev/openjev` is close on quality and **CC-BY-NC**. Not listed
  below as a default download.

## Adjacent / domain-specific system-1

Not a general Choice / Score / Noul peer. Do not rank these on
JevBench or pull them as a hosted-Jev substitute.

| Model | Download | Size | License | When it is / is not relevant |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| taiga-s1 | [`shhivv/taiga-s1`](https://huggingface.co/shhivv/taiga-s1) | 1.23M params; author: ~1 ms/decision on CPU | MIT (card) | FreeCAD PartDesign **executor**: a planner supplies an ordered feature goal; this model picks the next available FreeCAD command from live session state. No LLM, no vision, no screenshots. Relevant for CAD tool execution. Not a general JevBench / Choice / Score / Noul router. |

Hub card (author numbers, CAD-only, not JevBench): trained from
scratch on 24k synthetic sessions (~590k decisions); supervised,
then two DAgger rounds. Completes held-out longer goals than the
5-feature training cap (card: 100/100 eleven-feature goals; 95% of
seventeen-feature). With 20% random actions injected, 86–100% of
parts still finish depending on length. Scope is PartDesign
(sketch, pad, pocket, hole, revolve, patterns, mirror, fillet,
chamfer, shell). Numeric values come from the goal; the model
chooses the command. A later accessibility-tree executor is
described as future work, not a shipped sibling.
