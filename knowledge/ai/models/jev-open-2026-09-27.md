## What it is

A snapshot of **downloadable** System One / Jev-class decision
models as of 2026-09-27. Official TypeSafe Jev remains API-only and
is not listed as a download.

JevBench composite (Intelligence, Calibration, Speed, Cost) is the
independent board. Public-slice accuracies and author benches are
marked as such. Do not treat an author number as a board rank.

## When to read

Picking a local typed-decision model for NVIDIA or Apple Silicon.
Re-fetch the live board before any adoption decision.

## State of play (2026-09-27)

TypeSafe Jev 1.13.0 is still the hosted reference (`jev-latest`).
On JevBench v1.4.2.2 it sits **#4 at 63.29**. Three downloadable
open rows sit above it on that composite:

| Rank | System | JevBench Score |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Imajev-4B | 67.37 |
| 2 | Plumb-4B | 65.84 |
| 3 | decider-4b v2 | 64.13 |
| 4 | Jev 1.13.0 (hosted) | 63.29 |
| 5 | JevK5 v0.2.0 | 62.04 |

The board had 95 systems / 91 ranked in that revision. Highest
Intelligence on the board is still large generative models; the
harmonic mean does not let raw accuracy buy back weak Speed or Cost.

Community chatter (X + Hub, last ~10 days):

- Imajev-4B and Plumb-4B are the rows people cite when they say an
  open 4B beats hosted Jev on the **composite** board.
- Winnow-12B Q8 is the row people cite for a public-231 **tie** with
  Jev 1.13 (198/231). Composite rank is lower because of size/cost.
- CLM-8B is the loud new name. Frozen 8B + small heads. Treat claims
  as author-side until a JevBench row lands.
- Laya and Julia-1 are the viral tiny models. Fast. Weak on hard /
  many-label slices versus the 4B class.
- `openjev/openjev` is close on quality and **CC-BY-NC**. Not listed
  below as a default download.

## Hardware cheat sheet

- **8 GB / CPU:** Laya, Julia-1, Tiny-Jev, Kev-0.8B, Decider-0.8B/2B
- **12–16 GB NVIDIA:** Decider-4B, JevK5, Plumb, Imajev, Nox-4B, Winnow-E4B, Kev-4B
- **24 GB:** Winnow-12B Q8, Cygnet, Jev-Omni, Kev-9B, CLM-8B, Lux-9B
- **80 GB class:** Kev-27B bf16, Decider-35B-A3B
- **Apple:** Ollaya + Laya or Winnow-E4B; Kev MLX (0.8B/4B); Imajev ships `mlx/`; Tiny-Jev has an MPS path
