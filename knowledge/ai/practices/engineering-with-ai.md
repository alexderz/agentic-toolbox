## What it is

A dated survey of public CAD, EDA, simulation, MCP, skill, paper, and
local-model sources for mechanical and electrical engineering. The
long form lives in `docs/engineering-with-ai/`. This note is only the
pointer.

## When to read

Read the survey index before searching again for a FreeCAD or KiCad
MCP server, a text-to-CAD benchmark, or a small Verilog or CAD
checkpoint. Open the linked repository or paper before use. Nothing
in the survey is vendored or cleared for install.

## Unknown

Fields the survey leaves unknown stay unknown here. In particular:
general-model VRAM, Zoo kernel location, GD&T checker behavior, and
any benchmark this pass did not rerun.

## Do not

Do not install a third-party skill or MCP server from the survey
without intake. Do not copy the survey into a `SKILL.md`. Do not treat
author-reported scores as measurements from this repository.
